Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sourcing, cold milling, packaging, and orders.

What spices are available in the Tumic Spices collection?

Our pure range currently includes: Chilli Powder (from ₹45), Garam Masala (from ₹100), Biryani Masala (from ₹130), Turmeric Powder / Haldi (from ₹35), Coriander Powder / Dhaniya (from ₹35), and Aachar / Sabzi Masala (from ₹35). Each is available in 100g, 250g, 500g, and 1kg packs.

How does cold-grinding preserve the spices?

Industrial high-speed milling generates extreme friction heat that vaporizes delicate essential oils and scorches natural colors. Tumic Spices uses low-temperature slow micro-milling, preserving 100% of authentic aroma, flavor, and natural ASTA pigments.

How are online orders confirmed and synchronized?

When you checkout on the Tumic website, your order is automatically confirmed and recorded in Odoo Enterprise Invoicing with a verified tax invoice. You receive instant confirmation and can chat directly with our team on WhatsApp for delivery status.
